# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Traveling high-net-worth clients booking a Miami weekend in advance — bachelor and bachelorette parties, birthdays, corporate retreats, and similar group occasions. They're arranging logistics from out of town and want one trusted point of contact rather than juggling separate yacht, car, and nightlife vendors.

## Product Purpose

Avori Group is a single concierge for private yacht charters, exotic car rentals, and VIP nightlife access across Miami. It exists to remove the coordination burden of booking a luxury weekend across multiple unrelated vendors. Success is a fast, qualified inquiry through the contact form that converts into a booked weekend.

## Positioning

One concierge, arranged through a single line of contact, coordinates yachts, cars, and nightlife end-to-end — so nothing falls between providers the way it does when a trip is pieced together from separate booking sites.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: **Request Availability** (routes into the contact form, pre-filled with the relevant service).
- Secondary CTA: **Explore Fleet** / **Inquire** — a lower-commitment path for visitors not ready to submit an inquiry yet.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: *"The Miami you were promised."*
- Belief ladder, in order: (1) this one concierge can genuinely deliver yachts, cars, and nightlife together, not just one of the three; (2) they respond fast and reliably — inside the hour; (3) the roster is discreet and personally vetted, not a public marketplace; (4) booking is as simple as naming the weekend and letting the concierge build the rest.
- Proof on hand: none yet. No testimonials, press mentions, or client logos are collected currently — treat this as an open gap for a future `harden` or `onboard` pass once material exists.

## Brand Personality

Discreet, confident, fast. The voice is polished and economical — short declarative lines ("Curated, not catalogued," "Tell us the weekend. We'll build the rest.") rather than adjective-stacking. Confidence is shown through specificity (response times, named venues, named vehicles), not superlatives.

## Anti-references

No specific sites were named to match or avoid. The site's established navy/gold editorial-luxury direction (deep ocean navy primary, sand neutrals, restrained gold accent, serif display type) is the confirmed look — treat it as the baseline to preserve, not to be displaced by generic gold-and-black "luxury template" clichés (heavy gilt ornamentation, glitter, superlative-stacked copy) that would undercut the discreet, understated tone.

## Design Principles

- **One concierge, zero seams** — every touchpoint should reinforce single-point coordination across yachts, cars, and nightlife; never let a flow feel like three separate vendors bolted together.
- **Curated, not catalogued** — present the roster as personally vetted, not a marketplace. Avoid e-commerce patterns (heavy filtering UI, price-compare grids, review aggregation) that read as self-serve booking rather than concierge service.
- **Speed as proof** — the hour-fast response promise is a real differentiator; any inquiry or booking path should stay frictionless enough to not contradict it.
- **Discretion by default** — avoid patterns that imply public exposure (user-generated review walls, social feeds, unsolicited "as seen on" logo walls) unless the client explicitly opts in.
- **Insider confidence, not shouting** — copy and visuals project understated confidence; resist gaudy or ostentatious luxury signaling in favor of specificity and restraint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for hero reveals and hover transitions. Dark hero overlays (image + gradient + white text) must maintain AA contrast — the existing text-shadow treatment on hero copy should be preserved or strengthened, not removed, when adjusting hero imagery.
